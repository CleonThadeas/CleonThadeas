### 🚀 About Me

> *"Building useful products, solving real-world problems, and continuously evolving as an engineer."*

Hello! I'm **Andri Dwi Somantri** (widely known as **Cleon Thadeas**), a full-stack & mobile developer and product builder based in Bandung, Indonesia.

- 📱 **Mobile & Systems Specialist**: I focus on crafting fluid, responsive mobile experiences with **Flutter & Dart**, backed by scalable, high-performance backends written in **Laravel & Golang**.
- 💡 **Product & Business-Driven Mindset**: Having served as **Director of Production** at YouthMind Company (Student Company) and interned in IT operations at **Hotel Mercure Bandung Nexa Supratman**, I don't just write code—I engineer solutions that solve practical operational challenges and deliver clear business value.
- 🧪 **Founder of [AndrixLab](https://andrixlab.me)**: My personal digital lab and ecosystem where I experiment with modern web frameworks, explore **Generative AI** workflows, and launch innovative software products.
- 🎓 **Roots**: Software Engineering (Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak) graduate from **SMKN 11 Bandung**.
